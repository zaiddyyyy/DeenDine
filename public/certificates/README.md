# Certification photos

Drop a photo of a restaurant's halal/Zabiha certification here, named after
its restaurant `id` from `lib/restaurants.ts` (e.g. `al-bawadi-grill.jpg`),
then set that restaurant's `certificateImage` field to
`"/certificates/al-bawadi-grill.jpg"`.

Until a restaurant has `certificateImage` set, its "View certification"
popup shows a "coming soon" placeholder instead.
